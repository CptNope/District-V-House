A new website, ready to show

A redesign pitch for Jamie Harrity Burke’s home store in the historic Manchaug Company Store, Sutton, Massachusetts. Everything on the current site is here, rebuilt as a storefront window that changes with the seasons, with her own photos throughout.

By Jeremy Anderson Tech · November 2026

What’s here

Example site

Home, Christmas at House, The Shop, Vendors, Privacy and Terms: the full site with her content, working hours sign and season switcher.

Brand book

The idea, story, fact sheet, voice, logo rules, photography and the full library of her 46 photos.

Design system

Four season palettes with contrast checks, type, spacing, live components and the token files.

Audits & notes

Content audit, WCAG 2.2 AA, design taste and responsive audits, and the WordPress build notes (on GitHub).

The current site today

What a customer runs into on districtvhouse.com right now (checked November 23, 2026).

Security warning on every visit

districtvhouse.com and www.districtvhouse.com fail with a certificate error, so browsers warn people away before the page loads.

The old domain is parked

districtvdesign.com shows a GoDaddy “parked free” page, but it is still the website on Yelp, Facebook, Pinterest and directories.

Wrong hours for search and maps

The site’s machine-readable data and its map widget say Monday–Friday 9–5. The store is open Thursday–Saturday 10–5 and Sunday 11–4.

An empty Shop page

The online store page shows only the menu and footer.

Pages that say “coming soon”

Holiday’s at House still says the 2026 collection is coming while Christmas is open; Privacy Policy and Terms are placeholders.

Hard to read on phones

Pinch-to-zoom is turned off, and the season’s events are stacked in one long page with no structure.

  1. Build the WordPress block theme from this design system, with the four seasons as style variations.
  2. Fix DNS and the certificate on districtvhouse.com; send districtvdesign.com to it.
  3. Move the photos into the media library and correct the hours on Google Business Profile.
  4. Add an events list Jamie can post to herself, a working contact form, and real Privacy and Terms pages.
  5. Decide on the online shop, and confirm vendor names and the wall decals.
