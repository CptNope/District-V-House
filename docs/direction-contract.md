## Direction contract
THESIS: The homepage is the storefront of the old Manchaug village store at dusk: a transom with gilt sign-painted lettering, three lit display panes, a painted bulkhead holding hours and the door. Restyled every season, like the floor. Refuses the cream-and-script farmhouse boutique and the white-space luxury template.
OWN-WORLD: Dark tinted window glass carrying the season (holiday: evergreen-black glass, gold leaf, cranberry, frost white); thick sash mullions as the grid; gilt lettering with painted drop shade and keyline; enamel-like hanging OPEN/CLOSED sign; string price tags for the collective. Gloock for gilt, Hanken Grotesk for set text.
STORY: Visitor learns what this is (designer-run vendor collective in a historic mill-village store), that it's open now or when, and how to get there; then that it changes every season, that it's a women-led collective, that Jamie also designs rooms, and that the Manchaug Stroll is the December night to come.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Header sash with badge left, nav, hanging live OPEN sign right. Full-width transom with arched gilt "District V House" over "Manchaug Village · Sutton, Mass." Below, three photo panes in mullions with gilt captions. Bulkhead: today's hours + Get directions (primary) + Call.
