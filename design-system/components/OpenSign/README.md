# OpenSign

The hanging OPEN/CLOSED sign on two chains, top right of the header sash. It reads the real time in America/New_York against the store hours (Thu–Sat 10–5, Sun 11–4) and shows **Open · until 5 pm** on `ornament` or **Closed · Opens Thursday at 10 am** on `glass-2`. 2px `leaf` border, `radius-sign`, `shadow-sign`, `status` word in Gloock. It swings once on load (off under reduced motion), links to the Visit section, and is `aria-live="polite"`.

Keep hours in one place in code and in the schema.org data.
