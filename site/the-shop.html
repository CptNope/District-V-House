A one-of-a-kind shopping destination in Sutton, Massachusetts

Where vintage charm, beautiful home décor and the magic of Christmas come together.

Nestled in the historic Manchaug Village of Sutton, Massachusetts, District V House is a unique shopping destination filled with character, creativity, and unexpected treasures.

Located inside the historic Manchaug Company Store overlooking the Mumford River, our thoughtfully curated multi-vendor marketplace brings together an ever-changing collection of antiques, vintage finds, distinctive home décor, furniture, artisan-made goods, seasonal decorations, and unique gifts.

Unlike traditional retail stores, District V House offers an immersive shopping experience where every room tells a different story. Wander through beautifully styled spaces, discover one-of-a-kind pieces, and find inspiration for creating a home that feels collected, welcoming, and uniquely yours.

Our independent vendors bring their own creativity, personal style, and carefully selected merchandise together under one roof. From timeless antiques and vintage furnishings to beautiful new accessories, handcrafted gifts, and seasonal collections, there is always something new to discover.

Whether you’re searching for a statement piece of furniture, a meaningful gift, beautiful home accents, or simply an inspiring afternoon of shopping, District V House is a place worth exploring.

The store

District V House is located in the historic village of Manchaug in Sutton, Massachusetts. Once a booming mill village from the 1800s, it was home to the Manchaug Company Store. Our store is in the village store, being used for its original purpose. It is a quintessential New England hidden gem: a destination store, one mile from Route 146 and near 290 and 395.
