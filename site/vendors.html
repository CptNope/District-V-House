Vending at District V House

We’re at max capacity for vendors.

Thank you for your interest in vending. Thankfully, at this time we are at max capacity for vendors. We really appreciate your interest and would still love for you to come shop, although we are not accepting new vendors right now.

Vending is a great way to try selling your products without having the commitment of being in store. Although the commitment still exists: vendors are asked to update their spaces once a week.

Who can vend?

Only home decor vendors. The focus of this retail store is anything and everything home design decor. Home lifestyle:

  • Furniture and furnishings
  • Art and accessories
  • Plants and lighting
  • Rugs and hardware
  • Vintage and antique
  • Anything and everything home!

You must have an active Instagram account showcasing your goods and how you curate. Multi-level marketing products are prohibited.

Joining the wait list

We have a wait list of over 200 vendors, thankfully! To be added to the wait list, please fill out the application found on Instagram (the link is in our bio). You will not be contacted unless we have a spot open that is a perfect fit.

One floor, a whole collective

“Alone we are but one drop. Together we are an ocean.”

Jamie Harrity Burke, owner

Nearly all of our vendors are women-owned small businesses: antique dealers, pickers, artists and makers who share one floor in the old village store. Their styles are chosen to complement each other, not compete. On the floor you’ll find Birch Alley, The Modern Manor House and many more.
