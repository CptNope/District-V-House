One floor, a whole collective.

“Alone we are but one drop. Together we are an ocean.”

Jamie Harrity Burke, owner

District V House is a multi-vendor marketplace: a small, exclusive group of antique dealers, pickers, artists and makers, nearly all of them women-owned small businesses, under one roof. Every vendor refreshes their space each week, and the styles are chosen to complement each other, not compete. On the floor you'll find Birch Alley, The Modern Manor House and many more.

V

Born in District 5.

Worcester Manchaug

The V is Worcester's District 5, where Jamie Harrity Burke was born and raised. She's a self-taught designer who has been arranging rooms since she was four or five. Through District V Design she works with residential and commercial clients and gives each space a story about the people in it.

The store grew out of the studio. One designer can only take on so many rooms, so House puts her eye on every shelf for everyone else.
